What is Digital Harassment?

Digital harassment refers to bullying, threats, stalking, or abuse carried out through online platforms such as social media, emails, messaging apps, and gaming communities.

Common Types of Digital Harassment

  • Cyberbullying
  • Online stalking
  • Hate speech
  • Fake profiles and impersonation
  • Sharing private information without consent
  • Threatening messages

How to Prevent Digital Harassment

Protect Your Privacy

Use strong passwords and limit personal information online.

Block and Report

Block abusive users and report harmful behavior immediately.

Think Before Sharing

Avoid posting sensitive photos, addresses, or personal data.

Stay Respectful

Promote kindness and positive communication online.

Need Help?

If you or someone you know is experiencing online harassment, talk to a trusted adult, teacher, counselor, or contact online safety organizations for support.
